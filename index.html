What is Connect?

Connect is a National Cancer Institute (NCI) initiative to build a large research resource to study cancer development. Participants from diverse backgrounds are recruited through integrated healthcare systems across the United States, making Connect one of the most comprehensive cancer prevention cohort studies ever undertaken.

Study Highlights

Prospective Design

Participants are enrolled before cancer diagnosis and followed over time, enabling causal inference and early detection research that retrospective studies cannot provide.

Diverse Enrollment

Intentional recruitment across race, ethnicity, age, and geography ensures the study reflects and benefits the full U.S. population, including historically underrepresented groups.

Rich Data Resource

Biospecimens, questionnaire data, EHR linkage, and wearable device data are integrated into a secure, cloud-based platform accessible to the research community.

Access Connect Data

Approved researchers can access de-identified Connect data through the NCI Cancer Research Data Commons. Explore the data dictionary, request access, and begin your analysis.
