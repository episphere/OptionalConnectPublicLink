Data Access

Connect data are available to qualified researchers through the NCI Cancer Research Data Commons (CRDC). Data access follows a tiered model based on the sensitivity of the data requested.

Aggregate summary statistics and enrollment demographics are publicly available without registration. These include participant counts by site, demographic distributions, and biospecimen availability summaries.

View Summary Stats

De-identified individual-level data are available to researchers who register through the NCI CRDC and agree to the data use agreement (DUA). Registration requires institutional affiliation and IRB documentation.

  • Survey data (questionnaire responses)
  • EHR-derived variables (diagnoses, medications, labs)
  • Cancer registry linkage data

Genomic and other multi-omic data require a separate data access request (DAR) submitted through dbGaP. Review is conducted by the NCI Data Access Committee (DAC).

  • Germline whole genome sequencing
  • Methylation arrays
  • Metabolomic and proteomic assay data
Apply via dbGaP

Platforms

Connect uses a suite of interconnected, open-source platforms developed collaboratively between NCI's Episphere group and health system partners. All platform code is publicly available on GitHub.

Participant Portal

A web-based application where participants complete enrollment, surveys, and manage their study participation. Built with vanilla JavaScript and Firebase.

  • Online enrollment and consent
  • Survey administration (MyConnect)
  • Biospecimen appointment scheduling
  • HIPAA-compliant data handling

Site Manager Dashboard

An operational tool for health system site coordinators to manage participant recruitment, track biospecimen collection, and monitor study progress.

  • Recruitment tracking and reporting
  • Biospecimen workflow management
  • Participant verification and lookup
  • Real-time enrollment dashboards
View on GitHub →

Data Platform (epidataplatforms)

A researcher-facing data discovery and access platform, enabling exploration of available variables, cohort summaries, and data request initiation.

  • Variable browsing and search
  • Cohort summary statistics
  • Data availability by collection wave
  • Integration with CRDC access workflows
Visit platform →

Biospecimen Inventory System

Tracks the collection, processing, storage, and distribution of blood, urine, saliva, and tumor biospecimens from Connect participants.

  • Tube-level inventory tracking
  • Cold chain management
  • Aliquot and shipping workflows
  • Integration with LIMS systems at sites

Biospecimen Information

Connect collects biological samples at clinical sites affiliated with each health system partner. Samples are processed locally and shipped to a central NCI biorepository for long-term storage.

Blood
  • EDTA plasma
  • Serum
  • Buffy coat (DNA)
  • PAXgene RNA
  • Whole blood (methylation)
Urine
  • First morning void
  • Metabolomics aliquots
  • Microbiome pellet
Other
  • Saliva (microbiome)
  • Mouthwash rinse
  • Tumor tissue (incident cases)

Researchers interested in requesting biospecimens for ancillary studies should contact the Connect Biospecimen Working Group through the NCI CRDC data request portal.

Data Governance

Connect's data governance framework ensures participant privacy, regulatory compliance, and equitable data access.

  • IRB oversight: The study protocol is overseen by the NCI Special Studies IRB. Each health system also maintains local IRB approval.
  • Informed consent: All participants provide electronic informed consent prior to enrollment. Consent is tiered and covers survey data, EHR linkage, biospecimen collection, and future contact.
  • Data use agreements: Researchers accessing individual-level data must sign a DUA restricting re-identification, data sharing, and commercial use.
  • Security: Data are stored in FISMA-compliant, FedRAMP-authorized cloud infrastructure. Access is role-based and audited.
  • Equity in access: Connect prioritizes enabling researchers from underrepresented institutions through streamlined access pathways and technical support.
